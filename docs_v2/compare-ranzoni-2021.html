1. Protocol vs GitLab / paper

minF [ −α ∑i Ii Fi + (1−α) ∑i,j Rij Fi Fj ]
Q = (1−α)R − α diag(I)

Math meaning: Ii is discrete mutual information of gene i with fetal PAGA pseudotime; Rij is pairwise redundancy. α trades “track the HSC→lineage axis” against “do not pick a correlated globin/ribosome block.” A negative energy that beats the empty mask and the best singleton is a local minimizer of that panel design, not a claim of causal stem-cell biology.

5. Kaiwu CIM (not run) — what to expect

  • Same Q, n=5,000. Needs KAIWU_USER_ID / KAIWU_SDK_CODE. Classical Kaiwu tabu is already done.
  • Success is not “find k=50.” Tabu already did. CIM is a hardware compare: energy vs −0.7813, |F*| within tol of 50, overlap with Ocean/Kaiwu tabu.
  • Expect more membership jitter than v1. v1 Ocean∩Kaiwu was 48/50; here 31/50. This Q is flatter. CIM may look more like Ocean (lineage-heavy) or Kaiwu (HLF/HOXA9) or a third set. That is scientifically useful if energy is as good or better.
  • Do not expect to beat LASSO MSE. Eq. (4) is not Ridge. A “good” CIM result is a sparse negative-energy mask that still contains MLLT3/HOPX/SPINK2/NPR3 (their HSC story) without exploding |F*|.
  • A miss (huge |F*| or E ≫ 0) is an engineering/physics constraint at this p, not a failure of the fetal biology.

6. If this looks good — bio / health / ARES

  • Biology: Independent confirmation that a ~50-gene information panel can encode the published fetal HSC→lineage PAGA axis, overlapping their stemness names without their 1,000-HVG autoencoder.
  • Health: Compact, orderable cassette (qPCR / targeted RNA / perturbation library) for hematopoietic state, with built-in de-redundancy. Relevant to HSC fitness, reconstitution, and early myeloid/lymphoid bias — still leaky and not a clinical test.
  • ARES (aerospace / space-health): Radiation and microgravity hit the HSC compartment first. A fetal-rooted, low-redundancy HSC cassette is a candidate minimal observable for in-flight or analog blood profiling (few probes, high information). CIM would matter here only as a path to re-solve the same Q when panels are re-fit on new cohorts — not as a requirement to “discover” k=50.

7. What this run can and cannot say

  • Can: Unconstrained Eq. (4) on their published T returns |F*|≈50, negative energy, and HSC-root genes from their paper.
  • Cannot: Reproduce their AE embedding, organ-specific DE, ATAC-RNA integration, or cell-type RF/DNN accuracies. Ridge MSE is leaky.
