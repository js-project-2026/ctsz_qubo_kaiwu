5. What this run can and cannot say

  • Can: The pipeline is in the paper’s protocol family (residuals, ~5k genes, trajectory T, unconstrained QUBO + tabu). Fractional Q∩LASSO (~50%) is not wildly unlike their 44% at k=50.
  • Cannot: Replicate Table 1 (planted synthetic sources) or claim the hESC/PC9 unique-gene / GO results. Positive energy and |F*|≈p/2 mean the reported gene list is not a k=50 QUBO solution.
  • Next: Get |F*|=50 on p=5,000 (stronger α search, more tabu reads, or Leap hybrid), then overlap/enrichment. Until then the only healthy k=50 real-data mask in this project is the earlier 500-HVG RUNX1 residual run, which is a different T and pool.
